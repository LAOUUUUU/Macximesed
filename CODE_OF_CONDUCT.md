# Code of conduct

Macximesed is a small project, and this keeps it a good place to work.

## Expected
- Be kind and assume good intent. Plenty of people here are learning.
- Disagree about code, not about people. Explain why and point to evidence.
- Respect people's privacy. Don't ask anyone for their passwords, keys or personal details, and never post someone else's. If you share logs or paths, remove usernames and anything personal.
- Give credit for other people's work and findings.

## Not acceptable
- Harassment, insults, slurs, or discrimination of any kind.
- Personal attacks, trolling, or deliberately derailing discussions.
- Sharing someone's private information without their permission.
- Posting malware, stolen data or working exploits in public issues. Security reports go through [SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md).

## Enforcement
The maintainer can edit or delete comments, close issues and pull requests, or block people who don't follow this. To report a problem privately, use GitHub's **Report content** option on the comment or issue.

This code is based on the ideas of the [Contributor Covenant](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/).
